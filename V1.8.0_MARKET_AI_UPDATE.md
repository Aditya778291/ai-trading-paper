# V1.8.0 Market + AI update

## Added
- Market terminal in the Expo mobile app.
- Auto-refresh market snapshots every 5 seconds when browsing the main market list.
- Search across the built-in 63-instrument discovery universe.
- Indian/global indices, Indian equities, commodities, FX and crypto starter coverage.
- OHLC candlestick chart ranges: 1D, 5D, 1M, 6M, 1Y, 5Y.
- AI Intelligence endpoint and mobile AI screen.
- Technical AI context: RSI, MACD, SMA20/SMA50, trend and volatility regime.
- Existing ML research engine surfaced as an AI diagnostic when enough historical data is available.
- Existing paper-trading functionality retained.

## Important data note
The default adapter is Yahoo Finance. It can provide broad research data but is not an exchange-certified tick-by-tick feed and can be delayed or rate-limited. For guaranteed real-time exchange data across a complete market universe, configure a licensed market-data provider/broker feed.
